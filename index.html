Preprint

Implicit Causal World Models (ICWM)

Addresses a foundational limitation in model-based reinforcement learning where agents' internal simulators fail by conflating statistical correlations with true causal mechanisms. Drawing inspiration from implicit surfaces in geometric modeling, ICWM approximates the underlying Structural Causal Model from limited multi-agent demonstrations without requiring a pre-defined causal graph. Introduces a novel Multi-Agent Sequential Backdoor Criterion for causal imitation learning, deriving identifiable implicit causal world models from multi-agent observational trajectories.

Energy-Based Transfer for RL (EBTL)

🏛️ Published at 5th Conference on Lifelong Learning Agents (CoLLAs), 2026

Adapts supervised energy-based models to sequential decision-making. Formulates an energy landscape to perform in-support versus out-of-support detection at the boundaries of an agent's learned dynamics, serving as an energy-based gating mechanism to filter out harmful prior knowledge during skill transfer. Provides explicit energy regularization over high-dimensional state tensors to isolate positive transfer performance under severe covariate shift.

📄 Read arXiv:2506.16590
Preprint

Certified Landmarks (CAIRN)

Certified Approximation for Interpretable Representer Landmarks brings causal landmark attribution to label-free self-supervised learning using low-rank Neural Tangent Kernel (NTK) approximations. CAIRN's core novelty lies in propagating parameter sketching and landmark sampling uncertainties directly to the model's output, enabling rigorous interpretability guarantees.

📄 Read arXiv:2609.38901

📖 Tutorial Part 2: Semantic Surface Segmentation from LiDAR

DECEMBER 12, 2020 · IVPR GROUP, JADAVPUR UNIVERSITY

Real time Geometry Extraction and Vectorization of Objects from Point-Cloud Data (PART 2)

In this continuation tutorial, we address the challenge of semantic surface labeling on extracted geometric proposals. The pipeline was developed to operate online during LiDAR rotations with minimal computational overhead.

Synthetic dataset in BlenSor with multi-surface annotations
Comparative segmentation results vs PointNet & PointNet++
Classification pipeline using Random Decision Forests

Methodology & Results:

  • Synthetic Simulation: Created 32 complex environments using the BlenSor package simulating a Velodyne 32E sensor with calibrated Gaussian noise.
  • Classifier Optimization: Evaluated Random Decision Forests (RDF) and Extremely Randomized Trees (ERT), yielding high F1-scores without GPU dependencies.
  • Real-time Benchmark: Delivers >60% Mean Intersection over Union (MIoU) at 8–8.7 frames per second (115–124 ms latency), executing concurrently with spinning LiDAR sweeps.

📖 Tutorial Part 1: Real-time Geometry Extraction from LiDAR

DECEMBER 12, 2020 · IVPR GROUP, JADAVPUR UNIVERSITY

Real time Geometry Extraction and Vectorization of Objects from Point-Cloud Data (PART 1)

Details the mathematical formulation and algorithms for extracting coherent geometric surfaces from raw spherical coordinate point clouds generated by spinning laser rangefinders.

Pipeline block diagram from LiDAR sweep to vectorized mesh
Surface normal calculation and DFS inductive segment labeling
Spherical ordered grid representation

Core Algorithmic Principles:

  • Ordered Spherical Grids: Point clouds are ordered as P = {p(θ, φ, r)} along horizontal sweeps, allowing instant neighborhood connectivity without expensive KD-tree lookups.
  • Sub-Sampling for Noise Suppression: Sub-sampling along horizontal angles eliminates spurious normals caused by closely packed noisy points.
  • Homogeneity Propagation: Segment proposals propagate through a component-wise normal similarity threshold using inductive depth-first traversal.

In our research, we configured BlenSor to simulate a rotating 32-channel Velodyne HDL-32E sensor across diverse synthetic urban and indoor environments.
